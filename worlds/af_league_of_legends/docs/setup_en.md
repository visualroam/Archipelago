# AF League of Legends – Setup

Manual world: no client, no Riot API, nothing touches the game (Vanguard-safe).
Items are permissions: you may only queue in unlocked **roles** and only pick unlocked **champions**.
Traps are rules for the next match (no Flash, off-role, …).

Checks are marked by moderators in the AF dashboard. Collect enough Hextech Crystals and unlock every role, then win the finale.
